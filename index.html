    A CLEARER WAY TO EXPLORE

    Find a useful place to start.

    ZENVORA brings public web search, your own saved website shortcuts, and a small hand-picked directory together in one place. Search is powered by a web search provider when enabled by the site owner; ZENVORA does not create or endorse the sites that appear in results.

    GETTING STARTED

    How to search with ZENVORA

    1. Choose a search type. Use All for web and saved matches, Websites for website matches, or Videos for saved video links.
    2. Describe what you need. Enter a topic, question, or website name. Public web results are available when the site owner has configured the search provider.
    3. Save useful destinations. Sign in to add website addresses to your private account library. Your saved links are searchable from the Websites tab.

    ZENVORA is an independent project, not affiliated with the third-party services listed above. External destinations have their own operators, policies, and privacy practices. Review those terms before sharing information or creating an account on an external site.
